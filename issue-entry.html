Tareeqa (Instructions in Roman Urdu) Har item ki "Ending Balance" column mein dekhein — yeh system ke mutabiq is waqt store mein maujooda stock hai. Jo maal jahan bhej rahe hain uski quantity usi column mein likhein: Kitchen ke liye "Kitchen Issue", BOH ke liye "BOH Issue", FOH ke liye "FOH Issue", Bar ke liye "Bar Issue", aur Delivery ke liye "Delivery Issue". Ek item aik se zyada jagah bhi ja sakta hai — bas har column mein uski apni quantity daal dein. Jo item issue nahi ho raha, uska box khali chhor dein. Sab likhne ke baad neeche "Save Issues" button dabayen. Agar dobara isi din kuch aur issue karna ho to wapas aa kar naya amount likh dein, yeh purana entry ke upar nahi likhega balke ek nayi entry ban jayegi (neeche "Issued Today" mein sab dikh jayega).

Halka grey number (box ke andar) Kabhi kabhi khali box mein ek halka grey number nazar aayega — yeh system ka apna andaza hai ke aaj kitna issue lagna chahiye (purane din ke data aur is waqt floor par maujooda stock se calculate hota hai). Yeh sirf ek hint hai, khud se bharta nahi aur save bhi nahi hota — aapko apni marzi se asal quantity type karni hai. Abhi data sirf chand dinon ka hai, is liye yeh andaza thoda kam theek ho sakta hai; jitna zyada din ka data ikatha hoga, utna behtar hota jayega.
