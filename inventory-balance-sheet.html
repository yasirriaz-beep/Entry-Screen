Tareeqa (Instructions in Roman Urdu) Yeh report sirf dekhne ke liye hai, ismein kuch likhna nahi hai. "Beginning" wo balance hai jo pichli date se chala aya, "Add" purchases hain, "Sub" wo maal hai jo issue hua, aur "End" system ka calculated number hai. Agar kisi item ki rate recently zyada badli ho to "Rate Δ" column mein nishan (⚠) dikhega (purani rate dekhne ke liye us par mouse le jayein) — is par nazar rakhein, yeh ghalti bhi ho sakti hai aur sacha price change bhi. Upar "Food Cost" / "Non-Convertible" / "Uncategorized" tiles batate hain kitna stock bikne ke kaabil hai aur kitna sirf consumable/non-sellable hai. Date badalne ke liye "As of date" use karein.
Monthly view is restricted — only Directors and people explicitly granted "Inventory Balance Sheet — Monthly" can see it.
"Beg Adj" / "Add Adj" / "Sub Adj" are one-time reconciliation corrections tied to the owner's Excel ledger for a given month — they don't change any purchase or issue record, they just bring that month's Beg/Add/Sub/End in line with the Excel INVENTORY tab. Most rows will show "—" (no correction needed).
